We hop on a video call or go live. You watch us pick items from our stash in real time. You guide us, you approve the final picks. It ships within 24 hours of the call.

Place your order and we handle everything. Our team curates a box themed to your tier, seals it with mystery tape, and delivers it to your door. Pay only when it arrives.
